About

Research focus

My work sits at the intersection of applied machine learning and computer networks. I build systems that make sense of network traffic: classifying flows, detecting anomalies, and analyzing behavior in real time. I care about taking that work from a research paper to something that actually runs at scale in production. Along the way I've picked up a broad ML toolkit spanning NLP, reinforcement learning, and computer vision, applied across research internships in four countries and nearly three years as a software engineer at Cisco.

Current focus

At UNSW, I'm building fast, scalable, commercial-grade network traffic analyzers. My current work is on detecting AI agent usage in large-scale network traffic and inferring what tasks those agents are actually performing.

    Precise Measurement of Real-Time Video Conferencing User Experience with Only Volumetric Characteristics

    A machine-learning based traffic analysis method to measure per-channel media quality of video conferencing sessions (Zoom, Teams, Meet) from encrypted network traffic alone.

  2. ACM SIGMETRICS 2025

    FastFlow: Early Yet Robust Network Flow Classification using the Minimal Number of Time-Series Packets

    A reinforcement-learning-based network traffic classifier built for large-scale deployment.

  3. IEEE MSN 2021

    FlowFormers: Transformer-based Models for Real-time Network Flow Classification

    An efficient data structure for network traffic classification.

  4. ACPR 2019

    Meaning Guided Video Captioning

    A novel approach for training video captioning models.
